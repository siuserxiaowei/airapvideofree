## The Master Formula for Rap Video Prompts

To consistently generate compelling rap videos, structure your text prompts using this five-part formula:

```text
[Subject & Identity] + [Attire & Styling] + [Rap Performance & Rhythm] + [Environment & Lighting] + [Camera Physics & Aspect Ratio]
```

- **Subject & Identity:** Specify gender, age, expression, and posture (`charismatic underground rapper`, `focused gaze`, `chin slightly tilted upward`).
- **Attire & Styling:** Hip-hop aesthetics require clear textures (`black matte technical puffer`, `diamond Cuban link chain`, `dark rectangular sunglasses`, `vintage leather bomber`).
- **Rap Performance & Rhythm:** Tell the AI how to move (`rhythmic head nodding on 140 BPM tempo`, `confident street swagger`, `hand gestures emphasizing lyrical syllables`).
- **Environment & Lighting:** Build atmosphere (`monochromatic orange booth`, `cold wet asphalt with neon puddles`, `flickering sodium vapor streetlamps`).
- **Camera Physics:** Guide the lens (`8mm extreme fisheye`, `fast whip pan`, `subtle dolly zoom vertigo effect`, `808 bass micro-shake`).

## Essential Camera Movement & Lighting Cheatsheet

Use these technical descriptors in your prompts to dictate camera behavior:

| Camera Movement Tag | Visual Effect | Best Used For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `8mm fisheye lens, barrel distortion` | Extreme wide-angle with curved edges | Gritty underground drill & 90s Hype Williams videos |
| `Dolly zoom, vertigo z-axis compression` | Background warps while subject stays fixed | High-impact beat drops and shocking lyrics |
| `360-degree rotational orbit` | Camera smoothly circles around the performer | Bridge or climax of the song |
| `Low-angle hero perspective` | Camera placed near ground looking upward | Establishing power, confidence, and dominance |
| `Whip pan and rapid snap-zoom` | Fast blurry camera turns on snare hits | Fast-paced trap and drill transitions |
